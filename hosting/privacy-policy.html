Hydrio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Overview

Hydrio ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal hydration tracking application developed by Lumedev. This policy explains what data we collect, why, how it is stored, and your rights regarding it.

Hydrio is suitable for users of all ages and is offered as a free app with optional premium features. By using Hydrio you agree to the practices described here.

Data We Collect

Hydrio uses Google Firebase as its backend infrastructure. Data is processed under Google's Privacy Policy, which you can review at policies.google.com/privacy.

Account information

  • Anonymous identifier: Automatically generated when you first open the app. Required for the app to function.
  • Email and display name: Only if you choose to sign up with email/password or Google Sign-In. You can use the app fully without signing in.

Hydration data

  • Water intake records (amount, timestamp)
  • Daily goal, quick-add presets, reminder settings
  • Optional profile information: body weight, activity level, weather adjustment preference

Premium status

  • Which Premium products you own (managed by Google Play Billing)
  • Subscription start date and plan type

Diagnostics and analytics (via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics)

  • Anonymous app usage events: screen views, water logged, goal reached
  • Crash reports and stack traces
  • Device model, OS version, app version, language
  • An app-instance identifier assigned by Firebase

How Data Is Used

  • App functionality: store your hydration history, sync across devices when you sign in, restore premium status
  • Account management: let you sign in/out, recover access, link an anonymous account to a permanent one
  • Diagnostics: identify and fix crashes and bugs
  • Analytics: understand which features are used and improve the app over time

We do not sell your data, share it with advertising networks, or use it for marketing profiling.

Where Data Is Stored

  • Firebase Authentication stores email and authentication tokens on Google servers.
  • Firestore stores hydration and profile data on Google servers in the southamerica-east1 region.
  • Local device storage caches your data for offline use and stores per-device preferences (theme, text size, language, onboarding state).

All transmission between the app and Google servers is encrypted (HTTPS).

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, hosting.
  • Google Play Billing — Processes premium subscriptions and one-time purchases. We never receive your payment information; that is handled entirely by Google.

Your Rights

  • Access: Your data is visible inside the app at any time (History, Stats, and Account screens).
  • Correction: You can edit or remove any entry directly from the app.
  • Account deletion: Email us at lumedev.co@gmail.com with the email address linked to your account and we will delete it together with all associated data within 30 days.
  • Data export: Premium users can export their hydration data as JSON or CSV directly from the History tab.
  • Opt out of analytics: Disable diagnostic data sharing on your device via Android Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Internet: Required for sync, authentication, billing, and analytics.
  • Notifications: Used to send hydration reminders. You control this in app settings.

Children's Privacy

Hydrio is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has shared personal data with us, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data Retention

Account and hydration data are kept while your account is active. If you delete the account, data is removed within 30 days. Anonymous accounts that have not been opened for 12 months may be deleted automatically.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when we add new features that involve data. Changes are reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the new policy.

Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns: lumedev.co@gmail.com
