Hydrio — Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Hydrio account and the data associated with it.

Who this applies to

Hydrio is developed by Lumedev. This deletion process applies to any account created within the Hydrio Android app (package com.hydrio.app).

How to request deletion

Send an email to lumedev.co@gmail.com with the subject line "Hydrio account deletion request" and include:

  • The email address used to sign in to Hydrio (if you signed up with email or Google).
  • Or, if you used the app anonymously, the User ID shown in Settings → Account.

We will reply within 5 business days to confirm receipt and complete deletion within 30 days, in line with applicable data protection laws.

Don't have access to your account email anymore? Send the request from any email — we'll verify ownership through additional signals (purchase history, device, etc.) before deleting.

What data is deleted

On request, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email, name, anonymous ID)
  • Your hydration history (water entries, daily goals, achievements, statistics)
  • Your profile data (body weight, activity level, weather adjustment)
  • Your premium status record in our database
  • Any feedback messages associated with your account

What data may be retained

The following may be kept for legal, security, or operational reasons:

  • Payment records: If you purchased Premium, Google Play retains the transaction record under their own policy. We do not have access to your payment information.
  • Anonymized analytics aggregates: Aggregated metrics (e.g. "10,000 users opened the app today") that cannot be linked back to you may be retained for product improvement.
  • Crash reports: Anonymous crash logs older than 90 days may be retained for stability analysis.

Self-service options

You can also reduce the data we hold without requesting full deletion:

  • Sign out of your account in Settings → Account → Sign out. You'll continue to use the app as an anonymous user. Your cloud data stays linked to the previous account.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data from your device. (Cloud data remains until you request deletion.)
  • Edit or remove individual hydration entries directly in the app.
