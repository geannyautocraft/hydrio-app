Hydrio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

Overview

Hydrio ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal water intake tracking application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

Hydrio does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All data you enter in the app (water intake, goals, weight, preferences) is stored locally on your device only. We do not have servers, databases, or any infrastructure that receives your data.

Data Storage

Your data is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This includes:

  • Water intake records
  • Daily goals and preferences
  • Achievement progress
  • App settings (language, theme, reminders)

If you uninstall the app, all data is permanently deleted.

Third-Party Services

Hydrio uses Google Play Billing for premium purchases. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google. We do not have access to your payment information (credit card, billing address, etc.).

Analytics

Hydrio does not use any third-party analytics services. Basic usage events (such as water logged, goal reached) are stored locally on your device for your own insights and are never transmitted.

Permissions

  • Internet: Required only for Google Play Billing (premium purchases).
  • Notifications: Used to send hydration reminders. You control this in app settings.

Children's Privacy

Hydrio is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
